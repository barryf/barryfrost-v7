- I was back to work after last week's [holiday](https://barryfrost.com/2022/04/week-33-sprinklers). Not too many non-spam emails to read, but plenty of [Slack](https://slack.com/) activity to catch up on. Slack is undoubtedly king at our hybrid company. 
- One of my engineering managers is now on paternity leave for a couple of months so I'm looking after his team in his absence. It's a well-run team and I'm aiming to return the reins to its manager in a good state in June.
- I disappeared down a nineties music and football hole on Monday with the BBC's [The Birth of Cool Britannia](https://www.bbc.co.uk/iplayer/episode/m0016dtb/rock-family-trees-the-birth-of-cool-britannia) documentary which largely focuses on early Suede, Elastica and Blur. Followed by [Arsène Wenger: Invincible](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Arsène-Wenger-Invincible-Arsène/dp/B09K4W5PP6) on Arsenal's greatest manager. I won't apologise for enjoying every second of an evening's nostalgia.
- Three losses in a row. Let's not talk about Arsenal lately, though, OK?
- We had a barbecue on Friday with some neighbouring families. While the adults enjoyed the Pimm's and sunshine, the children bounced on the trampoline and played Minecraft. 
- My parents visited for Easter Sunday. The boys had an egg hunt in the garden, expertly planned by L, while I cooked roast pork. It seemed to go down well. Crackling, a cider gravy and then apple pie and custard. No need for chocolate after all that.
