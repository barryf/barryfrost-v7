- The under-sevens were short a football coach again for this week's training so I was asked to step in. I quite enjoy it. The children are (mostly) too little to answer back and just love running around together. A few even listen and remember to pass and find space.
- With the slightly warmer weather, training is back on grass instead of astroturf, requiring football boots not trainers. I bought my last boots when at university, so I needed to visit SportsDirect to buy a new pair. I picked out [these Nike Tiempo Legend beauties](https://www.nike.com/gb/t/tiempo-legend-9-academy-mg-multi-ground-football-boot-85FHMV/DA1174-705), in an eye-wateringly bright yellow/green colour. H was a bit jealous, but scored four goals in his simple black boots anyway.
- I drove back to my hometown, Chelmsford, for my Dad's birthday [lunch](https://barryfrost.com/2023/04/gy2dgblyw3h) on Thursday. The children (and L) were at school so it was just my parents, my sister and me. It's rare that it's just the four of us. I caught up on family news and introduced my mum to [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/).
- We had a lovely day out in London on Saturday for my in-laws' 50th wedding anniversary. The boys always enjoy a train ride and joined their cousins (L's brother's children) at [Camley Street Natural Park](https://barryfrost.com/2023/04/nq8qdj9o5ce) before heading for pizzas at [Fatto](https://barryfrost.com/2023/04/rpowgiq0e4). Ice creams, [Samsung gadgets](https://www.samsung.com/uk/explore/kings-cross/), crafts and a [playground](https://barryfrost.com/2023/04/a43smj1ooke) visit rounded out the day.
