I went to the recording of (probably) the [last two episodes of Richard Herring’s Leicester Square Theatre Podcast](https://www.richardherring.com/warmingup/16/11/2026/index.html) with Andy on Monday. RHLSTP (“RHLSTP!”, as you’re supposed to always shout in response) has been running for over 14 years; I’ve been a few times and listened to many more. Tim Vine was first to be interviewed, and was followed, appropriately, by fellow comedy podcaster and repeat guest, Adam Buxton. Adam played voice messages from other ex-guests, and then performed a little whimsical song. Richard understandably seemed a little overwhelmed by it all, and shuffled off after a standing ovation.

As a politics wonk, I was a bit jealous of H’s class trip to the Houses of Parliament, especially as his mother got to tag along as a parent helper. They had a full tour, although couldn’t visit the Commons Chamber because of [PMQs](https://www.parliament.uk/visiting/visiting-and-tours/watch-committees-and-debates/prime-ministers-questions/). I’m hoping his brother gets a turn another year and I’ll insist on helping out. 

Recommended: we finished the excellent second series of [This City Is Ours](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt31438328/) and I’ve started reading [Cory Doctorow’s Enshittification](https://bookhive.buzz/books/bk_CW2Z0RwYlsAX7CIunXzQ).

I took H to football training midweek where they played a pre-arranged friendly match with the other under-11s team. H’s team lost, but the gap between teams was small, which is impressive considering his team is newly formed this season. Sunday’s league match was business-as-usual: a comfortable 4-2 home win.

My parents came down to the Lantern Stadium to watch H’s match with me, and afterwards, we joined the rest of the family for Sunday lunch at the [Robin Hood and Little John](https://robinhoodandlittlejohn.com). My roast beef was pretty good, but the boys described their burgers as the best they’d ever had: quite an accolade. 

Oh, and I’m another year older. Toot!
