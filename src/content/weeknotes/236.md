- Time's relentless march is now targeting my eyesight. I rarely reach for my glasses, except when reading in bed, but even looking at my laptop or phone during the day is noticeably easier with glasses. So on Saturday, I ordered a couple of extra pairs to keep around the house. Next purchase, a Zimmer frame.
