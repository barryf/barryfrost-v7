- I was back in our Cambridge office this week for a couple of face-to-face chats and a free lunch. The company are announcing back-to-office measures this year. I'm hoping to be granted an exception to remain mostly remote. Whatever happens it seems there's an increasing inertia towards office-working again 😢
- I had to ask H to repeat himself this week after saying he'd tried to ask "Cop-ilot" a question. We worked out he was referring to Copilot. Hey Microsoft, some free branding advice: Cop-ilot is much more fun to say.
- I've moved my primary domain and imported my email from Google across to [Fastmail](https://www.fastmail.com/). The only snag was I'd set some old labels in Gmail to be hidden from IMAP, meaning I missed some emails. It wasn't hard to re-import. I've really no complaints. I quite like their native macOS and iOS apps for email, although I still use the stock calendar apps.
- In other home tech hygiene news, I've bought and set up a [Yubikey](https://www.yubico.com/) as another form of MFA. It was easy to add it to 1Password, Fastmail, Cloudflare and AWS. I still have my phone for TOTP but this is another factor _just in case._

- Arsenal were impressive beating Inter away on Tuesday, and were then frustrating losing at home to Manchester United. Nails are being bitten.
