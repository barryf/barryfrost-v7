A first for me this week: a tiny gig inside a dry-docked boat. We joined about 30 other fans to see [Malcolm Middleton on The Golden Hinde](https://web.archive.org/web/20261111120129/https://www.goldenhinde.co.uk/whats-on/10-music/792-malcolm-middleton-arab-strap-live-aboard-the-golden-hinde), the recreation of [Sir Francis Drake’s famous galleon](https://www.goldenhinde.co.uk/) in Southwark. Malcolm played a beautiful acoustic set of favourites with a surprising amount of audience interaction. 

Our [HomeView](https://www.apple.com/uk/apple-homeview/) arrived. Unlike *people on the internet*, my expectations really weren’t that great. I wanted a device for the kitchen to play radio and podcasts, to show photos and the weather. Which it does nicely.The boys love it. However, Siri is as unpredictable/unreliable as ever. 

Swimming twice. No altercations witnessed this week, although there was almost lane-rage when the Water Babies class took up a third of the pool and we were all squeezed up.

H’s team recovered from a slow start to win 6-2 on Sunday. He cancelled out his sloppy own-goal with an equaliser and an assist.

We’ve taken the plunge and booked a fortnight in Japan next year. We’ve booked through an agency who have helped with the itinerary, local guides, advance tickets, flights, etc., but it’s still a daunting prospect I’ve filed away in my brain until we get nearer.
