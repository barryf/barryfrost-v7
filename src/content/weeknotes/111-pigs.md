- I had my first gig for a couple of months this week. I met Andy in Kentish Town for some beers and then saw [Pigs Pigs Pigs Pigs Pigs Pigs Pigs](https://www.last.fm/event/4748229+Pigs+Pigs+Pigs+Pigs+Pigs+Pigs+Pigs+at+O2+Forum+Kentish+Town+on+06+October+2023) (that's seven pigs) at the Forum. They were very loud, very shouty, good in parts, but not really my cup of tea. I now know that "Doom Metal" isn't for me.
- Still, a few nice pints out on a Friday night are always welcome. Less welcome was the unexpected (to me) train strikes, meaning a long detour to get home and a £30 taxi.
- This was the second gig this year, after Mogwai, that I've worn ear plugs. At my age, with super-loud bands, they're necessary.
- I put my football boots on and had a kickabout in the park with H on Sunday. C rode his bike on the path then had snacks on the picnic blanket with his mummy. 24C in October - I may even be a little sunburnt.
- And then a tense 90 minutes to end the week as Arsenal finally beat Man City in the league. It wasn't pretty, but who cares? The hope is back 😬
