- Luckily we have a smaller TV in the <s>family</s>kids' room. When the boys are in bed we first have to clear a space on their sofa before settling down. We've been watching Noel Fielding's [Completely Made-up Adventures of Dick Turpin](https://www.apple.com/uk/tv-pr/originals/the-completely-made-up-adventures-of-dick-turpin/). He's been building me some truly evil Mario levels and then being annoyed when I find a loophole and complete them - it still counts.

Too high, too low, too squidgy, too old-fashioned, too deep, etc. And all are too expensive. We also have colour swatches. This is now my life.
