- Monday was a bank holiday: a welcome four-day work week.
- I took the boys to see the Super Mario Galaxy Movie at the local independent cinema. The film was better than I'd heard, although I'm a sucker for Mario nostalgia, so was always going to love it. My two enjoyed it almost as much as the McDonalds after.
- I baked my best banana bread yet. The leftover bananas I used were extra mushy, which I think helped.
- We finished watching the new series of Beef - fun, but not quite as good as the first - and have just started on the latest The Pitt, which continues the first series' breathless, gripping pace.
- H has played a lot of after-school football this week, driven across the county by his mum for trials and taster sessions at potential clubs for next season. His current team lost their last league game away to local rivals, Welwyn Garden City, on Saturday, ending a very difficult season. Onwards.
- Meanwhile we rode an emotional rollercoaster watching Arsenal play West Ham. Their disallowed equaliser bounced us from despair to relief, made even sweeter by rival fans' grizzling. Two league games to go to win the Premier League, plus a Champions League final to come!
