Here are _five albums that shaped me_, inspired by [Olly's post](https://olly.world/5-albums)
via [Martin Schuhmann](https://martinschuhmann.com/5-albums).
To echo Olly's point, these aren't necessarily favourites, but were influential in my life.

1. Ten (1991) - Pearl Jam
2. Tragic Kingdom (1995) - No Doubt
3. Olympian (1995) - Gene
4. If You're Feeling Sinister (1996) - Belle and Sebastian
5. Transatlanticism (2003) - Death Cab for Cutie
