You can subscribe to my content and follow me in multiple ways.
Subscribe to a feed using RSS, JSON Feed, Standard Site or h-feed, follow
me on one of the AT Protocol services I use to create this website
([read how I do this](/colophon)), or follow my profiles elsewhere.

### Feeds

- [<RSSIcon class="w-4 h-4 inline-block mr-1.5 translate-x-px -translate-y-px" />RSS](https://aboutfeeds.com/)
  — [Articles and Weeknotes](/feed.xml).
- [<JSONFeedIcon class="w-4 h-4 inline-block mr-1.5 -translate-y-px" />JSON Feed](https://www.jsonfeed.org)
  — [Articles and Weeknotes](/feed.json).
- [<StandardSiteIcon class="w-4 h-4 inline-block mr-1.5" />Standard Site](https://standard.site)
  — Subscribe to my publications using a compatible Standard Site reader
  (I recommend [Standard Reader](https://standard-reader.app/about)).
- [<MF2Icon class="w-3.5 h-3.5 inline-block ml-0.5 mr-1.5 -translate-y-px" />h-feed](https://microformats.org/wiki/h-feed)
  — Microformats 2 `h-feed` markup is used on
  [Articles](/articles) and [Weeknotes](/weeknotes) pages.
  Subscribe with a compatible [reader](https://indieweb.org/reader).
