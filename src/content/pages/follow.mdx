You can subscribe to my content and follow me in multiple ways.
Subscribe to a feed using RSS, JSON Feed, Standard Site or h-feed, follow
me on one of the [atproto](https://atproto.com) services I use to
[build this website](/colophon), or find my profiles elsewhere.

### Feeds

- [<RSSIcon class="size-4 inline-block mr-1.5 translate-x-px -translate-y-px" />RSS](https://aboutfeeds.com/)
  — [Articles and Weeknotes](/feed.xml) or the full [Stream](/stream.xml) feed.
- [<JSONFeedIcon class="size-4 inline-block mr-1.5 -translate-y-px" />JSON Feed](https://www.jsonfeed.org)
  — [Articles and Weeknotes](/feed.json) or the full [Stream](/stream.json) feed.
- [<StandardSiteIcon class="size-4 inline-block mr-1.5" />Standard Site](https://standard.site)
  — subscribe to my publications using a compatible Standard Site reader
  (I recommend [Standard Reader](https://standard-reader.app/about)).
- [<MF2Icon class="size-3.5 inline-block ml-0.5 mr-1.5 -translate-y-px" />h-feed](https://microformats.org/wiki/h-feed)
  — Microformats 2 `h-feed` markup is used on all pages.
  Subscribe with a compatible [reader](https://indieweb.org/reader).
